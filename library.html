Protein library

Every protein our screen put through a literature check (the first 60, 27 added in wave 3, and 13 from a glial screen, both on 22 November 2026): what is known, which papers exist, what kind of evidence each paper is, and whether the protein still needs a focused study. Click a protein to open it.

Read this first. Computational hypotheses only, no discovery claims. Evidence labels marked manual were assigned after reading the abstract; labels marked auto come from simple rules on the title and abstract and can be wrong. For proteins with more than 40 PubMed records only the 8 most recent are listed; use the PubMed link for the rest.

How to read "study need": Needs mechanistic study = no focused experimental paper on how the protein works was found; Partly studied = some experimental work exists, but key questions are open; Being studied by others = knockout models or active groups already exist; Studied elsewhere = excluded from our portfolio because the protein is already characterised or unsuitable; Check needed = more than 40 papers exist and only the 8 most recent were labelled, so the protein still needs a manual literature review. Wave 3 proteins were added on 22 November 2026 by a rule-based shortlist and checked by PubMed volume only: Well studied = 100 or more PubMed records, so a mechanistic gap is unlikely. Wave 4 is a glial screen (oligodendrocyte, astrocyte and microglia genes); 10 of its 13 genes turned out to be well studied, which showed that our unstudied score does not work well for glia.
